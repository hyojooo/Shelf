Features

Built for the way you actually work

Quiet by default, powerful when you need it. Shelf stays out of your way until the moment you reach for something you copied an hour ago.

Automatic capture

Every text snippet and image you copy is saved in the background — no extra step, no manual "add to history".

Instant search

Fuzzy, real-time filtering across your entire clipboard history. Find that link or snippet in a keystroke.

Image preview & zoom

Thumbnails in the list; click to view the full-size original. Perfect for screenshots and diagrams.

Smart deduplication

Re-copying the same content refreshes its position instead of stacking duplicates. Your history stays clean.

Favorites

Pin what matters. Favorites are never touched by automatic cleanup, so your go-to snippets stick around.

Local & private

Your history lives only on your machine. Nothing is uploaded — ever. Older entries auto-prune to save space.

Keyboard first

One keystroke to everything
